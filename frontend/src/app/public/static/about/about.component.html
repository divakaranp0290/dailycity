About DailyCity

DailyCity is an independent city-focused information platform created to provide reliable daily updates for major Indian cities.

Our platform brings together essential information such as petrol and diesel prices, gold and silver rates, power cut updates, traffic conditions and Panchang timings in one easy-to-access place.

DailyCity is designed for residents, professionals and households who depend on timely city updates to plan their daily activities efficiently.

We continuously work to improve content accuracy, usability and overall user experience while keeping the platform simple and accessible.

Our Purpose

The purpose of DailyCity is to reduce the effort required to find daily city information from multiple sources by presenting it in a clear, structured and user-friendly format.
